# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Film, television, and commercial producers — specifically production companies, studios, networks, and ad agencies seeking authentic Black cultural music expertise for US-based productions. These are time-pressured creative decision-makers: TV executives, film directors, showrunners, and agency EPs. They arrive evaluating whether to hire this team. First impressions are decisive.

## Product Purpose

Position Black On Both Sides Music as the premier music supervision company for US-based Black productions. The site's sole conversion goal is compelling visitors to hit "Contact Now" — every section builds toward that action. Success is a TV exec or ad agency creative who lands on the page and immediately reaches out. This is a handoff artifact Everton Lewis Jr. presents to clients; it must hold up to professional scrutiny.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, Culturally Grounded, Cinematic. The voice is direct, proud, and without apology. Unapologetically Black — not performatively, but as a statement of expertise and identity. Emotional goal: the visitor should feel the weight of this team's credentials and cultural authority before they read a word.

## Anti-references

- Generic music industry agency sites — template-feeling, forgettable
- AI-generated slop layouts — the brand demands handcrafted precision, not the default output of design tools
- Royal or editorial serif aesthetics (Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display era) — too delicate and mismatched with the logo
- Corporate sterility — no navy/grey/teal combinations, no SaaS-pattern layouts
- Gold or purple accent colors — not in the BOBS palette
- Portfolio templates — this is a creative powerhouse, not a freelancer portfolio
- Wix-era results — animation-broken, structurally weak

## Design Principles

1. Culture is the foundation, not the accent — every design choice should communicate that Black cultural intelligence is the core competency.
2. Cinematic over corporate — the quality bar is a Marvel Studios title card, not a SaaS landing page.
3. Weight before elegance — the brand is bold and unapologetic; refine it, don't soften it.
4. Every section compels the next action — this is a single conversion funnel; each section should leave the visitor more convinced they need to call.
5. No apology in the craft — this site is handed to network executives; it must be visually and technically impeccable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. `prefers-reduced-motion` support on all animations. Color contrast: all text against backgrounds must meet AA minimums. No purely color-based state indicators.
